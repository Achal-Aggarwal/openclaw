# Warm chat mount-frame experiment

This temporary experiment uses the production Team-scale Control UI benchmark,
synthetic data, warm application/HTTP caches, and the live-fast latency profile.
Attribution captures use CPU sampling and LoAF script attribution separately from
unprofiled comparisons. The initial window's row count and row types must be
recorded with the phase breakdown (module evaluation, snapshot decode, component
creation, Markdown, style/layout).

A candidate must remove unnecessary work at its owner. Stop with attribution if
visible-row rendering dominates. Do not edit cached row-footprint ownership while
the related work is pending, sidebar presentation, composer, or pane-list RPCs.

The predeclared primary gate is mount-frame LoAF blocking at least 40 ms lower
at the median and better in at least 9 of 11 interleaved pairs on one quiet AWS
c7a.4xlarge (c7i.4xlarge fallback). Also report hello-handled, FCP, first painted
rows, final rows, CLS, and structural batches after first row on chat and dashboard.
First rows may rise at most 10 ms at the median. A secondary regresses with
non-overlapping IQRs, or at least 9 of 11 worse pairs and a median increase greater
than max(10 ms, 3%). Retain every per-pair delta. No private deployment captures
or content belong in this experiment.
