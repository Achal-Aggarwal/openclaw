## Scope and result

- Mainline scan: every commit on `main` from `beaee570a5d4` (2026-10-21, start of 2026.9.34 preparation; 2026.9.34 recorded no scan cursor) through `f227543e4815` (pinned 2026-10-25): **3,595** non-merge commits, classified in seven lanes (Control UI, agent runtime, Gateway/state, infra/CLI/config, plugins/channels, scripts/misc, and non-`fix` titles screened for embedded hotfixes).
- Secondary sources: open and recently closed P0/P1 issues and PRs reported against 2026.9.33/2026.9.34, the 2026.10.7 stable remediation set (#181553), and operator-requested PRs.
- Policy: every applicable P0; P1 fixes ported unless they need thousands of risky lines or main-only infrastructure; no new config keys, public Plugin SDK exports, schema/protocol changes, or dependency changes.
- Result: the branch carries the product fixes and repairs listed below, plus the release-retrospective batch 5 tooling (#181920). Every product port has a regression test that fails without its product change and passes with it, except where noted.
